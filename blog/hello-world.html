Welcome to Yeah, I'd Print That

October 29, 2026 · 2 min read

[PLACEHOLDER] Welcome! This is Yeah, I'd Print That: a place to see what a 3D printer can make, and to find out that it's a lot less complicated than it looks.

Why this site exists

[PLACEHOLDER] Most people have heard of 3D printing, but very few have watched it happen up close. The goal here is simple: show the prints, explain how they're made in plain English, and make the whole thing feel like something anyone could try.

What you'll find here

  • The gallery: printed artwork, with what each piece is made of and how long it took.
  • How it's made: 3D printing explained from file to finished object.
  • This blog: behind-the-scenes builds, experiments and the occasional spectacular failure.

If you've ever looked at something and thought "I wonder if I could print that," you're in the right place.
